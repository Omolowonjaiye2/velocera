# VÉLOCÉRA image sources

This portfolio prototype uses real Lamborghini photography from Pexels for visual consistency. VÉLOCÉRA and all model names/specifications in the app are fictional.

Pexels states that its photos are available under the Pexels license and can be used for personal and commercial purposes. Always review the current license before a commercial launch.

- Lamborghini photography search: https://www.pexels.com/search/lamborghini/
- Lamborghini Aventador photography: https://www.pexels.com/search/lamborghini%20aventador/
- Lamborghini Revuelto photography: https://www.pexels.com/search/lamborghini%20revuelto/
- Individual Pexels photo used in the hero set: https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-lamborghini-car-on-the-road-9814983/

The exact image CDN URLs are centralized in `src/data/cars.js`, so they can be replaced later without changing the components.
